# Safelight Corporate Contributor License Agreement ("Agreement")

**v1.0**

Thank you for your interest in contributing to Safelight ("the Project"),
founded and principally authored by Anthony Reimche ("the Maintainer").

This Agreement is for **corporations and other legal entities** ("the
Corporation") whose employees contribute to the Project as part of their work.
It allows the Corporation to authorize its designated employees to submit
Contributions on the Corporation's behalf. Individual contributors who are not
acting on behalf of an employer should instead sign the [Individual CLA](ICLA.md).

Like the Individual CLA, this Agreement exists to keep the Project's **dual
licensing** model possible — GPL v3 for open-source use, plus a separate
commercial license — by ensuring the Maintainer holds sufficient rights in every
Contribution. The Corporation **retains ownership of its copyright.**

Please read this document carefully before agreeing to it. To complete the
Agreement, an authorized representative of the Corporation should sign via the
process described at the end of this document and provide the schedule of
authorized contributors.

any subsidiary or affiliate that the Corporation controls.

**"Contribution"** means any original work of authorship, including any

inclusion in, or documentation of, any of the products owned or managed by the
Project. "Submitted" means any form of electronic, verbal, or written
communication sent to the Maintainer or the Project, including but not limited to
communication on source-code control systems, issue trackers, and pull requests,
but excluding communication that is conspicuously marked or otherwise designated
in writing by You as **"Not a Contribution."**
