# Licensing

Safelight is **dual-licensed**. You may use it under either of the following,
and you choose which one fits your situation:

1. **The GNU General Public License, version 3** (the open-source option), or
2. **A commercial license** from the author (the proprietary option).

Both cover the same software. The difference is the obligations that come with
each.

## 1. Open-source use — GPL v3

By default, Safelight is free software under the [GNU GPL v3](LICENSE), together
with a Section 7(b) **attribution-preservation term** (see the [NOTICE](NOTICE)).
Under this license you may use, run, study, share, modify, sell, and build upon
Safelight for any purpose, **provided you comply with the GPL** — most notably:

- If you distribute Safelight or a derivative work, you must make the complete
  corresponding **source code** available under the GPL v3.
- You must preserve the author attribution required by the Section 7(b) term.
- You may not impose further restrictions beyond those the GPL allows.

This is the right choice for open-source projects, personal use, and anyone
comfortable with copyleft.

## 2. Commercial use — proprietary license

If the GPL's obligations do not work for you — for example, you want to:

- ship Safelight (or a derivative) inside a **closed-source product**,
- distribute it **without releasing your source code**, or
- obtain it under **terms negotiated for your organization** (warranty,
  indemnity, support, removal of the attribution requirement, etc.)

then you can purchase a **commercial license**, which releases you from the GPL's
copyleft obligations for the covered use.

## How this is possible — the CLA

Dual licensing only works if one party holds enough rights to offer the software
under both licenses. The author holds the copyright in the original work, and
every external contribution is made under a **Contributor License Agreement
(CLA)** that grants the author the right to license contributions under both the
GPL and commercial terms — while the contributor keeps ownership of their
copyright.

If you want to contribute, see **[CONTRIBUTING](docs/dev/contributing.md)** and
the CLA documents:

- [Individual CLA](CLA/ICLA.md) — for personal contributions
- [Corporate CLA](CLA/CCLA.md) — for contributions made on behalf of an employer

Signing is automated: a bot will prompt you on your first pull request.
