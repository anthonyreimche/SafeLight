Safelight uses the standard **inbound = outbound** model: when you contribute
code or documentation, you license your contribution under the same **GPL v3**
that covers the project, and you keep the copyright to your work. No separate
contributor agreement is required. See
[CONTRIBUTING](docs/dev/contributing.md).
