# Support Safelight

Safelight is a free, open-source RAW photo editor, built and maintained by one
person, [Anthony Reimche](https://github.com/anthonyreimche). It's free to use and
free to upgrade, and it always will be. There are no subscriptions, no "pro"
edition and no early-access builds. Everyone gets the same app.

Payment is never expected. If Safelight is useful to you and you'd like to say
thanks, a coffee is always appreciated:

- ☕ **[Buy Me a Coffee](https://buymeacoffee.com/anthonyreimche)**

Everyone who chips in is thanked in [SUPPORTERS.md](SUPPORTERS.md). If you'd
rather stay anonymous or go by a handle, mention it in your note.

## Other ways to help (free)

Money isn't the only way to help. All of these genuinely move the project forward:

- ⭐ **Star the repo**. It's the cheapest marketing there is.
- 🐛 **File good bug reports** and 💡 **well-scoped feature requests**.
- 🧩 **Build and share extensions**. Tag your repo `safelight-extension` and
  you'll be listed in [SUPPORTERS.md](SUPPORTERS.md) too.
- 📝 **Improve the docs**, write a tutorial, or make a video.
- 🗣️ **Tell other photographers** Safelight exists.
