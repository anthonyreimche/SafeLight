# Support Safelight

Safelight is a free, open-source RAW photo editor, built and maintained by one
person — [Anthony Reimche](https://github.com/anthonyreimche). The app is free to
use and free to upgrade, and it always will be. There's no paywall, no "pro"
edition, no feature held hostage.

That only stays true if development stays funded. Sponsoring Safelight pays for
the time it takes to build it, and it directly buys new features for *everyone* —
including people who never pay a cent.

## How your money turns into features: sponsorware

Most big features are released as **sponsorware**:

> A new feature is made available to sponsors first. Once it reaches its funding
> goal, it's released **free to everyone** under the GPL.

So you're never buying *access* — you're buying *timing*, and you're funding a
feature's release into the commons. Sponsors get it early; everyone gets it
eventually. Current sponsorware goals live on the
[roadmap](ROADMAP.md) and the GitHub Sponsors page.

## One-time support

Not into subscriptions? One-time contributions are just as welcome:

- **GitHub Sponsors** — one-time amounts available on the Sponsors page.
- **[Buy Me a Coffee](https://www.buymeacoffee.com/anthonyreimche)** — quick and casual.
- **Fund a feature** — put a one-time amount toward a specific
  [sponsorware goal](ROADMAP.md) and help push it over the line for everyone.

## Other ways to help (free)

Money is not the only currency. All of these genuinely move the project forward:

- ⭐ **Star the repo** — it's the cheapest marketing there is.
- 🐛 **File good bug reports** and 💡 **well-scoped feature requests**.
- 🧩 **Build and share extensions** — tag your repo `safelight-extension`.
- 📝 **Improve the docs**, write a tutorial, or make a video.
- 🗣️ **Tell other photographers** Safelight exists.
